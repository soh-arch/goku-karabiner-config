AbcAct
Keymap

Hold one key. The whole right hand changes meaning.

AbcAct is a layered keymap for macOS. Three hold-keys open three layers; inside the main layer, four Act keys under the left hand — a, s, d, f — combine freely into 16 tiers that redefine the right hand. The fingers never leave the home row.

01

Layers, Act Keys & Notation

Hold L-⌘ and the Asterisk layer is live for as long as you hold it. Add Act keys under the left hand to pick which of the 16 tiers the right hand is in. Tap a hold-key on its own and it types something instead.

The three layers
Hold L-⌘ Main
Asterisk Apps, cursor, editing, mouse, tabs, windows, symbols, function keys.
Hold L-⇧ Aside
Bra One-handed numpad. With Spacebar also held: drawing-app palette.
Hold R-⇧ Aside
Cket Volume, brightness, keyboard backlight, playback transport.
The four Act keys — left hand home row
Scale
a — Amplify Same action, bigger unit: word, paragraph, document, half-screen.
Target
s — Select Turns a movement into a selection, or a pointer move into a drag.
Remove
d — Delete Removes, reverts, or cuts away what the movement covered.
Shift focus
f — Focus Moves attention instead of text: pointer, tab, app, window.

Cmd+A → hHold L-⌘ and a, press h.

The same thing, as an indicator: dots are a s d f, lit = held.

10

Glossary

The handful of words this manual uses in a specific sense.

Layer

A whole alternate keyboard that is live only while its trigger key is held. Release the trigger and it's gone — nothing is ever "left on".

Asterisk / Bra / Cket

The three layers, triggered by holding L-⌘, L-⇧ and R-⇧ respectively. Asterisk is the main one; the other two are small and self-contained.

Act key

One of a s d f, held inside Asterisk. They stack freely, so four keys give 16 tiers.

Tier

One of the 16 meanings the right hand can have, chosen by which Act keys are held.

Amplify (a)

Same action, larger unit — character to word, line to paragraph, line end to document end, nudge to half-screen.

Select (s)

Turns a movement into a selection; with f, turns a pointer move into a drag.

Delete (d)

Removes what the movement covered; with s, becomes clipboard and undo territory.

Focus (f)

Moves attention rather than text — mouse pointer, tab, application, window.

Clip slot

One of 12 named clipboard buffers on the bottom row, stored on disk. Independent of Maccy's rolling history.

Alone / tap

Pressing and releasing a hold-key without touching anything else. Most trigger keys carry a tap action too, so few keys are spent on holding alone.
